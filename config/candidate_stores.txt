# Candidate retailers for `scripts/discover_stores.py probe`.

# NOT a tracked-store list — config/sites.yaml owns that. These are domains
# worth ASKING whether they stock Beyblade X, via their own site search.
#
# TWO RULES DECIDE WHAT BELONGS HERE, both from the user:
#
# 1. SHIPS TO SWEDEN, FROM THE EU. EU core (DE/NL/BE/AT/LU/FR) is the target
#    because freight is cheapest; Nordic neighbours equally good; rest of the
#    EU fine but slower. The US and UK are OUT — post-Brexit the UK is a third
#    country, so customs and freight erase any saving on a 130 kr top.
# 2. A WIDE SELECTION, CONSISTENTLY. A shop with three Beyblades is not worth a
#    request every 30 minutes. Judged on the product count `probe` reports.
#
# Removed 2026-09-09 under those rules, recorded so they are not re-added by a
# future search: beywarehouse.com, beysandbricks.com, beyblade-toys.com,
